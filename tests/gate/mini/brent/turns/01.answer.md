# Brent Group, WELL 30_6-5 — Porosity, Water Saturation, Hydrocarbon Potential

**Answer to the question:** Over the Brent Group stretch (2841.02–2957.91 m, the tops-file unit BRENT GP. 2841–2958 m), the 20 lithology zones Z1–Z20 cover 83.3629 m of the 116.8909 m gross interval (768 samples; 201 samples are outside any zone). Porosity is moderate: zone-pooled total porosity PHIT mean **0.1988 v/v** (whole interval 0.2005 v/v) and core-calibrated effective porosity PHIE_CC mean **0.1254 v/v** (whole interval 0.1206 v/v), with Z7 the best porosity zone (PHIT 0.2318, PHIE_CC 0.2186) and Z10–Z16 the poorest (PHIE_CC 0.0047–0.1628, many effectively zero). Water saturation is mixed: zone-pooled SW mean **0.7512 v/v** (whole interval 0.7783 v/v) and SXO mean **0.8013 v/v** (whole interval 0.830), but the sandy crossbedded and sandstone zones carry much lower SW (Z7 mean 0.2265, min 0.0954 at 2879.8816 m; Z5 mean 0.314, min 0.1503 at 2873.7854 m; Z4 mean 0.411). Net pay (SW < 0.7, VCL < 0.3) is flagged over **24.1% of the interval's 768 samples (185 samples, 28.1573 m)** — concentrated in Z4 (95.8% NET_PAY), Z5 (90.9%), Z7 (100%), Z9 (60%), Z1 (43.3%) — and the integrated screen promotes a **PRIME_OIL_TARGET over 21.6408 m (26.0% of the zoned thickness)**. Whether this is "hydrocarbon-bearing" is log-indicated potential, not a confirmed fluid: the geophysical hydrocarbon screen (neutron-density crossover / Vp–Vs) did not run because DTS is absent, MHI is capped at 1 as a limit/default in 62% of zoned samples, Z2/Z3 pay-adjacent flags sit in bad hole and cannot grade as hydrocarbon movability, and Z17 has no MHI and is unassessed. The pay flags are model outputs at salinity 30488.7162 ppm with no core-saturation validation over the Brent Group.

## Point 1 — Porosity and water saturation of the Brent Group; is any hydrocarbon-bearing?

**Finding.** The Brent Group is subdivided into 20 lithology zones (Z1–Z20) with mean PHIT between **0.05637** (2938.4031 m, Z16) and **0.4089** (2861.7458 m, Z3); zone-pooled PHIT is 0.1988 and PHIE_CC is 0.1254 (n = 567 of 768 samples; the 201 unzoned samples raise PHIT to 0.2005 and PHIE_CC to 0.1206). SW runs from 0.0954 (2879.8816 m, Z7) to 1.0 across 53% of zoned samples; SXO tracks SW closely (SXO = SW = 1 at 410 of 768 samples). Zone means place SW below the 0.7 pay cutoff in Z3 (0.6396), Z4 (0.411), Z5 (0.314), Z6 (0.6465) and Z7 (0.2265), and above it in Z1 (0.7787), Z2 (0.7428) and Z8 (0.7703). NET_PAY is set in 30.0% of zoned samples (170 of 567, 25.2532 m) and 24.1% of the whole interval (185 of 768, 28.1573 m), with the maximum zone shares in Z7 (100%), Z4 (95.8%), Z5 (90.9%), Z9 (60%) and Z1 (43.3%).

**Is it hydrocarbon-bearing?** The hydrocarbon analyzer flags **POTENTIAL_PAY over 25.3 m (30.3% of the zoned thickness)** and promotes **PRIME_OIL_TARGET over 21.6408 m (26.0%)** where POTENTIAL_PAY coincides with EXCELLENT_RESERVOIR (35.6% of zoned thickness: Z4–Z5, Z7, Z13, Z19). However, all four hydrocarber classes in the petrophysical screen are weak: MOVABLE_HC 2.48 m (2.97%), LOW_SW 0.435 m (0.521%), NO_INDICATION 55.2 m (66.2%). The geophysical screen returns NO_INDICATION over 83.4 m (100% of zoned thickness) but **VP_VS_RATIO is not computed** — DTS is missing and shear sonic is implausible — so the 100% NO_INDICATION is a default classification, not a negative finding. MHI (mean 0.8869 over zones, 0.8968 whole interval) is a model output capped at 1 as a limit/default in 62% of zoned samples, and it is NaN for Z17 (23 samples) where MOVABLE_HC was not assessed. Thus: pay-flagged, reservoir-quality sand exists over a thin set of zones, but hydrocarbon fluid presence is indicated by saturation routing and pay flags, not confirmed by any geophysical screen, and the Z2/Z3 MOVABLE_HC/MOVABLE flags (Z2 16.7% MOVABLE_HC, Z3 25% MOVABLE_HC + 5% LOW_SW) sit in bad hole and cannot grade as hydrocarbon movability.

Prime oil: The result sets **PRIME_OIL_TARGET at 174 of 768 samples, 26.51 m** (26.0% of zoned thickness), criterion POTENTIAL_PAY + EXCELLENT_RESERVOIR (21.6408 m); POTENTIAL_PAY alone spans 25.3 m over Z1 (2 runs), Z4 (3 runs), Z5 (1), Z6 (1), Z7 (1), Z8 (2), Z9 (1). Water-bearing dominates the rest (38.9% of zoned thickness), with NON_RESERVOIR 32.5% (Z2–Z3, Z8, Z10–Z12, Z16, Z18, Z20).

MHI: mean 0.8869 over zones (n = 471 of 567), 0.8968 over the whole interval (n = 635 of 768), min 0.3168 at 2879.8816 m (Z7), max 1. MHI = SW/SXO where SXO is from shallow resistivity; NaN where SXO is only SW^(1/5) — which is exactly the Z2/Z3 situation and part of the 23 samples with no MHI in Z17.

**Hydrocarbon analyzer sections.** (petrophysical): POTENTIAL_PAY 25.3 m; MOVABLE_HC 2.48 m (9 runs, Z2, Z3, Z4, Z6, Z8); LOW_SW 0.435 m (3 runs, Z3, Z6, Z8); NO_INDICATION 55.2 m (66.2%). **Limits and caveats.** Z2/Z3 MOVABLE_HC and LOW_SW/MOVABLE flags sit on bad-hole mud carry and cannot grade as hydrocarbon movability; the Z7 min MHI 0.3168 and Z1 max 1 are model outputs, and MHI max 1 in 62% of zoned samples is a limit/default. ## Point 5 — Trust in the readings: bad hole in Z2/Z3, the core-calibrated PHIE_CC transform, uncalibrated PHIE beside it

**Bad hole.** The bad-hole step (CALI−BS threshold 1.5 in, |DRHO| threshold 0.15 g/cm³) sets BAD_HOLE at **65 of 768 samples (9.903 m)** in the Brent interval, concentrated in Z2/Z3 (88.9% / 65% of their samples per the bad-hole record). Consequence: Z3's PHIT mean 0.3533, PHIE 0.1359, PHIE_CC 0.1454, SW 0.6396, SXO 0.9553 and the entire Z2/Z3 MOVABLE_HC/low-SW hydrocarbon flags carry the bad-hole penalty; VCL means (Z2 0.6742, Z3 0.8407) and BVW means (0.1075, 0.0927) are equally unreliable.

**Core-calibrated PHIE_CC.** PHIE_CC = PHIT_CC − (PHIT − PHIE), clipped to 0–PHIT_CC, with the all-depths correction **PHIT_CC = 1.136·PHIT − 0.039** (n = 200 plugs, r² = 0.55, recorded for the well). It is a whole-well linear fit; no zone-by-zone fit exists, so Brent-specific bias is unverified. Evidence of agreement: Z1's 7 plugs give PHIC mean 0.1417 and PHIE_CC-at-core-depths mean 0.1346 (median 0.1414), near the log mean 0.1264 with zero-value spread on both sides. Disagreement: Z18's single plug reads PHIC 0.043 against logs that show zero values — no agreement; plug counts run 1–35 per zone and core coverage across the Brent Group is not established from the visible core rows (fetch Z13–Z16 for the rest).

**Uncalibrated PHIE beside it.** PHIE is the porosity-step output with PHIE_CC in this interval; PHIE hits its minimum 0 at **17% of zoned samples** (PHIE_CC at 20%, PHI_SEC at 94%, VCL at 12%, BVW at 20%, PERM at 10% are similar limit/default floors). Where PHIE_CC is zero (Z17 entirely; many samples), the uncalibrated PHIE is a default, so the effective-porosity zero readings are tool floors, not necessarily tight-rock truth. The saturation step writes **SW = 1 at 3793 well-wide samples with porosity < 0.01 v/v**, so SW = 1 over Z10–Z16 and Z19–Z20 may be the floor rule rather than measured water.

**What the curves cannot show.** MHI is model output; PERM_CC is core-calibrated only where VCL ≤ 0.30 and clay cutoff 0.85 P95 permit, else Coates-PERM fallback; INVASION_RATIO rests on RSHA/RDEP because RXO is absent; no DTS means no velocity-derived saturation, crossover, or Vp/Vs hydrocarbon screen; no core SWC validation exists over the Brent Group (SWC at 74 of 768 samples, SOC at 44 — inputs, not validation).

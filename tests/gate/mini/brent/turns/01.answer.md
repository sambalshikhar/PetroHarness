# Brent Group, Well 30_6-5 — Porosity, Water Saturation and Hydrocarbon Indicators

## Answer

The Brent Group sampled in this well spans **2841.0193–2957.9102 m** (gross 116.89 m, 768 samples), subdivided into 20 lithology zones (Z1–Z20, 83.36 m, 71% of the interval).

- **Porosity:** Mean total porosity (PHIT) is **0.1988** over the 567 zoned samples and **0.2005** over all 768; mean core-calibrated effective porosity (PHIE_CC) is **0.1254** (zoned) and **0.1206** (whole interval). Zone means span a wide range — PHIE_CC from **0.0047** (Z11, silt) to **0.2186** (Z7, sand); PHIT from **0.0564** (Z16) to **0.4089** (Z3, coal). The most workflow-referenced curve is **PHIE_CC** (it drives reservoir quality grading and the NET_RES/NET_PAY cutoffs), but its calibration is weak (r² = 0.55) and it holds 0 in 20% of zoned samples (a limit/default value). **PHIT** is the most internally consistent curve across the whole interval but is uncalibrated and its spread is inflated by bad-hole readings.
- **Water saturation:** Mean SW is **0.6929** (median 0.7415) over the 567 zoned samples, **0.7182** over the whole interval, and **0.7893** in the 201 samples outside the lithology zones. SW reaches **1.0 in 36%** of zoned samples (a limit/default value). By zone, SW is lowest in **Z7 (0.2265)**, Z5 (0.314), Z4 (0.411), Z6 (0.5699), Z8 (0.5912) and highest in Z13, Z14, Z19 (1.0), Z15 (0.983), Z20 (0.9739), Z11 (0.876).
- **Hydrocarbon-bearing:** The data indicates **potential** hydrocarbon-bearing pay but does **not** confirm hydrocarbons. Pay flags and low-SW classes concentrate in **Z1, Z4, Z5, Z6, Z7, Z8, Z9** (zones pooled NET_PAY set in 30% of samples, 25.25 m of the 83.36 m of zones). Gas effects are weak (1.9% of zoned thickness), geophysical hydrocarbon indicators are uniformly absent (100% NO_INDICATION, CROSSOVER_FLAG = 0 samples), and MHI is a screening output resting on a thin resistivity sample base with no core fluid calibration. **No hydrocarbon presence is confirmed.**

**Which curve is most trustworthy:**
- **PHIE_CC** is the core-calibrated effective porosity (PHIT_CC = 1.136×PHIT − 0.039, n=200, **r² = 0.55**) and is the curve the reservoir-quality and pay classifiers grade on the results. It is therefore the operative curve, but the calibration is weak, and its minimum of 0 occurs in 20% of zoned samples — flagged as a limit or default value.
- **PHIT** is the most stable across the whole interval (0.2005 over all 768 samples, low p90 at 0.2633) but is an uncalibrated model output of the porosity step.
- **Bad-hole contamination:** BAD_HOLE is set at 65 of 768 samples (8.5%, 9.8931 m of the 116.89 m interval). Within the zones, Z2 is 88.9% bad-hole and Z3 65.0% bad-hole ("pad tools read mud"); both zones read high PHIT (0.3028, 0.3533) and high PHIN (0.4168, 0.4780) and low VCL (0.6742, 0.8407 — sand reading on mudded coal), which is why zone PHIE_CC lines sit high there (0.15, 0.1454).
- No core fluid plugs are exploitable for this interval (SWC 74 of 768, SOC 44 of 768, PHIC ~200 plugs well-wide), so no plug-by-plug porosity calibration is stated per zone; matrix endpoints depend on lithology-log assignment (coal has two matrix endpoints in the normalization record).

**Geophysical indicators:** **100% NO_INDICATION** — CROSSOVER_FLAG is set at **0 of 768 samples** (0 m). Vp/Vs-ratio screening could not be computed because shear slowness (DTE) reads ~1.5–2× DTC and is implausible in plausible slowness ranges; no artificial estimate was added.

**Gas effects:** WEAK_GAS_EFFECT (ND separation 0.03–0.08) only **1.64 m (1.9% of zoned thickness)** — Z1 (2842.6958 m), Z5 (2873.63–2874.09 m), Z7 (2883.996–2888.416 m). **NO_GAS_EFFECT over 98%**. Primary method ND separation; DRHO, sonic, PHIT_GAS_CORR were fallbacks.

**MHI:** Model output MHI = SW/SXO. Pooled mean **0.8778** (zones), 0.89 (whole interval), but MHI = 1 in **60% of zoned samples** (limit/default), min 0.3168. SXO is from RSHA at only 494 samples and forced to 1 wherever SW is 1 (274 samples), so MHI above ~0.9 is structurally expected in water-bearing stretches and is not a gas confirmation.

**Conclusion:** The Brent Group shows **petrophysical screening evidence of pay potential** — low SW and high MHI in Z1, Z4–Z9 — but **no geophysical gas indicator, no core fluid confirmation, and no verified hydrocarbon column**. NET_PAY and HCPT are cutoff flags: HCPT derives from the same SW cutoffs with no core fluid calibration and is a screening thickness, not a core-verified hydrocarbon body. Hydrocarbon presence is unconfirmed.

## Limitations

- **PHIE_CC** is a weak model correction (r² = 0.55, n=200); reservoir grades that depend on it (EXCELLENT_RESERVOIR etc.) grade PHIE_CC, PERM_CC and VCL jointly, so a single good interval can pull a mixed zone's share up.
- **Bad hole:** ~9.9 m of 116.89 m gross flagged (8.5% of samples); Z2 (88.9%) and Z3 (65%) carry mud-reading pads, inflating their porosity and mislabeling lithology.
- **No exploitable core fluids** for this interval (SWC 74/768, SOC 44/768); MHI algebra is forced to 1 on 274 water-bearing samples and rests on RSHA shallow resistivity at only 494 samples.
- **Missing measurements:** the well lacks **DTS and RXO** (neither in the input). Geophysical hydrocarbon indicators and VP/Vs screening are therefore uncomputable beyond the neutron-density crossover; geophysical evidence of hydrocarbons is absent by lack of data, not by measurement.
- **Curve limits/defaults:** PHIE_CC min 0 in 20%, SXO max 1 in 61%, SW max 1 in 36%, MHI max 1 in 60%, PHI_SEC min 0 in 94%, VCL min 0 in 12% — several are limits or defaults rather than measured values.
- **NET_PAY counts:** The exclusion-free recompute (25.9082 m pooled) runs slightly above the record's NET_PAY (25.2532 m) because it does not exclude coal and bad-hole samples; the difference is compositional, not a saturation-model conflict.

# Brent Group, Well 30_6-5 — Porosity, Saturation and Hydrocarbon Potential

The Brent Group in well 30_6-5 is 2841.0193–2957.9102 m (116.9 m gross, 768 samples). Twenty lithology zones (Z1–Z20, 83.4 m) were computed; the pooled zones carry **PHIT 0.1988**, **PHIE 0.133**, **PHIE_CC 0.1254 v/v**, and **SW 0.6929 / SXO 0.8013 v/v**. NET_PAY is set in 185 samples (28.19 m, ~24 % of gross) entirely inside the Z1–Z9 stretch, most of it in Z7 and Z5. The computed hydrocarbon analyzer labels a combined 25.0 m of the zoned thickness as PRIME_OIL_TARGET / POTENTIAL_PAY, but with no drilled fluid, no RXO and no DTS in the Brent, and with the MHI pinned to 1 wherever shallow-resistivity SXO lacks invaded-zone support (274 samples, / computed record), **the logs indicate pay-flagged, low-saturation, clean reservoir zones that are classified as oil-potential targets; none of it is confirmed hydrocarbon-bearing.**

## Point 3 — Hydrocarbon-bearing intervals, NET_PAY placement, MHI and geophysical indicators

**Finding.** NET_PAY is set only inside the Z1–Z9 pack. The pay samples (mean SW 0.336, mean PHIE_CC 0.2069, mean VCL 0.0671, mean PERM_CC 276.5 mD) cluster in **Z7 (13.2587 m of pay, zone-mean SW 0.2265, zone-mean PHIE_CC 0.2186, zone-mean VCL 0.0268), Z5 (4.566 m, zone-mean SW 0.314, zone-mean PHIE_CC 0.2081, zone-mean VCL 0.0557), Z1 (1.9786 m, pay-strip SW 0.5764, zone-mean SW 0.7765), and thin Z4, Z6 and Z8/Z9 slivers**.

- **The result (petrophysical hydrocarbon indicators):** POTENTIAL_PAY runs total 25.3 m (30.3 % of zoned thickness) across Z1, Z4, Z5, Z6, Z7, Z8, Z9; LOW_SW adds 4.06 m (4.87 %) and MOVABLE_HC (LOW_SW with MHI < 0.7) only 3.36 m (4.03 %); the rest — 50.7 m (60.8 %) — is NO_INDICATION.
- **The result (integrated hydrocarbon potential):** PRIME_OIL_TARGET (pay flag + EXCELLENT_RESERVOIR) totals 23.6 m (28.4 %, concentrated in Z1, Z4, Z5, Z7), POTENTIAL_PAY 1.61 m (1.93 %), WATER_BEARING 37.2 m (44.7 %), MARGINAL 4.06 m (4.87 %), NON_RESERVOIR 16.8 m (20.2 %).
- **MHI:** mean 0.8778 pooled over the zones (n 471 of 567) and 0.8074 inside NET_PAY (n 185). It **tops out at 1 and falls back to 1 wherever SXO lacks invaded-zone support (274 samples)**, so the “moveable hydrocarbon” signal is systematically capped and cannot be read as a strong hydrocarbon response (computed record).
- **Geophysical gas indicators:** the recorded limitations note thin WEAK_GAS_EFFECT streaks (1.9 % of zoned thickness in Z1, Z5, Z7 from ND separation 0.03–0.08 alone), an ND artifact, not gas.

**Why this is not a confirmation of oil or gas.** The labels are built from SW flagging plus reservoir-quality thresholds (class thresholds are computed defaults: PHIE 0.15/0.10/0.08, PERM 10/1/0.1 mD, VCL 0.25/0.4). MHI rests on shallow-resistivity SXO. There is **no drilled fluid, no RXO and no DTS in the Brent** (map / WHAT THE CURVES ARE). The ND-separation streaks are logged but are not corroborated by any fluid observation. Accordingly: the Brent carries clean, low-saturation, high-porosity pay intervals that the computed hydrocarbon analysis classifies as prime-oil-potential; **the fluid system itself is unproven.**

## Point 4 — Bad-hole conditions, coal cutouts, lithology and net-pay thickness

**Finding.** Net-to-gross over 117 m gross: NET_ROCK 66.6 m (56.9 %), NET_RES 62.94 m (53.8 %), NET_PAY 28.19 m (24.1 %). NET_PAY screening confidence: 26.21 m flagged as higher-confidence (not non-reservoir or marginal rock, bad hole ≤ 50 %, ≥ 1.0 m) and 1.981 m low-confidence (silt marginal). The loss chain is: **43 % of the interval fails the VCL < 0.3 clay cutoff; of the rock passing it, 5 % fails porosity/coal/bad-hole exclusions; and 55 % of that rock fails the SW < 0.7 cutoff**, so pay is the last, thinnest gate.

- **Bad hole** is flagged on 65 of 768 samples in the interval (9.9 m), and per the recorded limitations affects 88.9 % of Z2 and 65.0 % of Z3; density, neutron, PHIE and SW there are unreliable. Whole-well bad-hole exclusion removes 308.1 m of net pay (net-pay exclusion flags / recorded computation).
- **Coal cutouts** take two forms: Z3 (COAL_GROUP_4, 2.8955 m) is dropped by the lithology-based coal exclusion (47 samples, 1.1 m net reservoir / 0.1 m net pay), and an additional 345 samples from no-lithology coal readings (RHOB < 1.8 g/cm³, NPHI > 0.35) remove 51.5 m of net reservoir and 43.7 m of net pay (net-pay exclusion flags).
- **Lithology controls where the pay sits:** EXCELLENT_RESERVOIR (with 16.2 % NO_QUALITY_DATA) occupies 37.4 m (44.9 %) of zoned thickness, almost entirely the clean sand/crossbedded zones Z1, Z4, Z5, Z7, Z13–Z15, Z19; clay-bearing Z2–Z3, Z8, Z10–Z12, Z16–Z18, Z20 fall to FAIR/NON_RESERVOIR or NO_QUALITY_DATA.

**What the computed sections behind the flags say.** reports pay averages (PHIE_CC 0.2069, SW 0.336, VCL 0.0671, PERM_CC 276.5 mD) from the 185 NET_PAY samples. splits the zoned thickness into POTENTIAL_PAY (25.3 m), LOW_SW (4.06 m), MOVABLE_HC (3.36 m) and NO_INDICATION (50.7 m). upgrades 23.6 m to PRIME_OIL_TARGET and leaves 37.2 m WATER_BEARING, 4.06 m MARGINAL, 16.8 m NON_RESERVOIR. Because the integrated labels merge the SW cutoff with reservoir-quality classes, **WATER_BEARING on sandstone is a cutoff outcome (SW ≥ 0.7), not a lithology expectation**. The LOW_SW petrophysical runs (Z6, Z8, Z12; 9.4489 m) sit in silt zones that integrates as MARGINAL/WATER_BEARING — i.e., low SW in clay-bearing rock does not promote hydrocarbon flags.

**Limits on the net-pay number.** The NET_PAY SW/SXO/BVW stats use n = 170 of the 567 zoned samples, while the results count 185 flag samples / 28.19 m; the runs-call and flag counts differ as computed. Zone-run pay sums (28.1573 m) sit 2.283 m short of the whole-interval 28.19 m because the remainder lies in the 201 unzoned samples. Bad-hole exclusion counts are whole-well (12 631 samples); only the Brent runs are shown here. The entire flag machinery — NET_ROCK, NET_RES, NET_PAY, PRIME_OIL_TARGET, reservoir-quality classes — rests on computed thresholds (VCL < 0.3, PHIE_CC > 0.08 clastic, SW < 0.7, permeability and clay classes), not field-calibrated cutoffs.

## Limitations that bound the answer

- **Model outputs and fallbacks:** All porosity, saturation, BVW, MHI and permeability curves are model outputs of the computation. PHIE_CC is a weak same-well core transform (r² = 0.55) applied log-wide; VCL_ND is undefined above the N-D clay point, leaving clay tails on GR. SXO and MHI lean on shallow-resistivity (RSHA/RMED) proxies because no RXO exists; 274 samples carry SXO = 1 with SW, forcing MHI fallback to 1 (computed record).
- **Missing well tools:** No DTS, no RXO, no drilled fluid samples. Fluid identity cannot be confirmed or refuted from these logs alone.
- **Zoned vs whole-interval:** The 201 samples outside any zone (PHIT 0.2052, SW 0.7893, SXO 0.9108) are excluded from the zone-mean claims and contribute 2.283 m to whole-interval NET_PAY, so zone-level percentages understate pay if the unzoned part is geologically continuous.
- **Thinned sample base in pay:** NET_PAY samples in Z1–Z9 are 13 (Z1), 23 (Z4), 30 (Z5), 5 (Z6), 88 (Z7), 2 (Z8), 9 (Z9), so thickness is dominated by Z7 and Z5.

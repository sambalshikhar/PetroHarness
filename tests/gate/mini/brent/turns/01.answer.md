# Brent Group, Well 30_6-5 — Porosity, Water Saturation and Hydrocarbon Potential

## Point 1 — Porosity and water saturation of the Brent Group, and whether any is hydrocarbon-bearing

Across the Brent Group interval **2841.0193–2957.9102 m** (768 samples; 20 zoned intervals covering 83.3629 m of the 116.8909 m gross — 71%) (map), the formation is **porous but predominantly water-bearing**. Zones pooled (567 samples inside zone labels): **PHIT mean 0.1988 v/v, PHIE (uncalibrated) mean 0.1330 v/v, PHIE_CC mean 0.1254 v/v**; **SW mean 0.7512 v/v and SXO mean 0.8013 v/v**; **BVW mean 0.0763 v/v**. Whole-interval including the 201 unzoned samples: PHIT mean 0.2005, SW 0.7783.

Where SW routes below the 0.7 cutoff, NET_PAY flags fire: **170/567 zoned samples (30.0%, 25.2532 m)** and **185/768 whole-interval samples (24.1%, 28.1573 m)**. The clearest hydrocarbon-hint zones are **Z7 (Sand, 2877.5955–2890.8542 m: SW 0.2265, PHIE_CC 0.2186, 100% NET_PAY)**, **Z5 (2870.2803–2875.157 m: SW 0.314, PHIE_CC 0.2081, 90.9% NET_PAY)** and **Z4 (2865.8606–2869.366 m: SW 0.411, PHIE_CC 0.181, 95.8% NET_PAY)**. Integrated hydrocarbon assessment puts **PRIME_OIL_TARGET on 13 runs / 26.0% of zoned thickness (21.6408 m)** and POTENTIAL_PAY on 28.5%. However, no hydrocarbon fluid is confirmed: these are log-indicated pay/reservoir-quality flags, not production data. BVW reaches 0.2463 v/v zones pooled, and SW mean 1 spans Z10, Z11, Z12, Z13, Z14, Z16, Z17, Z19 and Z20, consistent with the water-bearing interpretation of most of the unit.

## Point 3 — Water saturation across the formation and where it hints at hydrocarbons

- **SW pooled (567 zoned samples): mean 0.7512, median 1, min 0.0954 (Z7), max 1**.
- **SXO in this interval is routed from shallow resistivity — RSHA/RDEP, since no RXO is measured — at 358 of the 768 samples; the interval record lists no SW^(1/5) fallback, while the whole-well record places 92 such fallbacks log-wide.** The interval record reads "SXO is from shallow resistivity at 358 samples; 1 where SW is 1 too (water-bearing, either route) at 410 samples" (map saturation record). SXO median is 1 and mean 0.8013, modestly above SW mean 0.7512 — a weak/partial flushing signal, not a uniform wash.
- **Where SW is low (below the 0.7 cutoff, hinting at hydrocarbon):** Z7 mean 0.2265 (min 0.0954 at 2879.8816 m), Z5 mean 0.314 (min 0.1503 at 2873.7854 m), Z4 mean 0.411, Z6 mean 0.6465, Z3 mean 0.6396. These are the zones where LOW_SW, POTENTIAL_PAY and PRIME_OIL_TARGET land.
- **BVW** (PHIE_CC × SW): mean 0.0763 v/v pooled; in water-bearing silts it collapses toward 0 (Z10–Z12: BVW 0.0246–0.068 with PHIE_CC 0.0047–0.0246), consistent with SW = 1 overriding the porosity.

## Point 4 — Hydrocarbon flags

- **NET_PAY:** 170/567 zoned (30.0%, 25.2532 m), 185/768 whole-interval (24.1%, 28.1573 m). Routes only through SW < 0.7 and NET_RES (VCL < 0.3, porosity above the lithology-family cutoff) after the net-pay step **excludes coal and bad-hole samples**, so the flag does not include the water-bearing bulk or the poor-hole silt/sand packages (map net_pay record).
- **PRIME_OIL_TARGET:** a screening flag (pay + EXCELLENT_RESERVOIR), not a direct fluid measurement; it inherits the SW < 0.7 cutoff (map net_pay record). Set on 174/768 samples, 26.51 m whole interval, 13 runs / 26.0% of zoned thickness (21.6408 m) — concentrated in Z4–Z5 and Z7.
- **Petrophysical indicators:** POTENTIAL_PAY on 9 runs (30.0% of zone samples); MOVABLE_HC on 9 runs (LOW_SW with MHI < 0.7 — Z2 2857.63110–2857.93579 m, Z3 2858.85034 m and 7 further runs not shown in the result); LOW_SW on only 3 runs (Z3 2861.74585 m, Z6 2876.68115 m, Z8 2893.44507 m); NO_INDICATION on 26 runs / 71.5% of zoned thickness (59.5885 m).
- **Integrated hydrocarbon potential:** WATER_BEARING 38.9% (32.4613 m), NON_RESERVOIR 32.5%, PRIME_OIL_TARGET 26.0%, POTENTIAL_PAY 9 runs, MARGINAL 3 runs.
- **MHI:** computed only where SXO comes from real shallow resistivity (map MHI record); values span 0.3168–1 across the interval at 635/768 samples. Because 410 samples have SXO = SW = 1 (water-bearing either route), MHI coverage over the flushed zone is weak — MHI cannot be used to argue for moveable hydrocarbons across the water-bearing bulk.
- **Gas indicators:** ND separation finds **WEAK_GAS_EFFECT on only 4 runs** (Z1 at 2842.69580 m; Z5 2873.63306–2874.09033 m; Z7 2883.99634–2884.14868 m and 2887.95874–2888.41577 m — 1.9% of zoned samples) and NO_GAS_EFFECT on 100% of zoned thickness (83.3629 m). This is the one gas flag with a plausible log basis (clean sandy lithologies, good hole), but it stays at the weak tier; the neutron-density crossover call finds nothing, a weak negative (limits, Point 3).
- **Geophysical hydrocarbon indicators:** read NO_INDICATION on 100% of samples **only because DTS is absent** — the samples are uncomputable, not ruled out (limits, Point 4).

## Point 5 — Data weaknesses and trust limits

- **Bad hole:** 65 of 768 samples flagged, 9.8931 m / 8.5% of gross, concentrated in Z2 (2.3029 m, 88.9% of that zone) and Z3 (1.8821 m, 65%). These are excluded from NET_RES/NET_PAY by VCL and bad-hole cutoffs, so porosity weakness does not leak into pay flagging — but Z2/Z3 porosity reads are unreliable.
- **Unzoned gap:** 201 samples sit outside any zone; the zone labels cover 83.3629 m of the gross 116.8909 m (map). Their means are degraded (PHIT 0.2052, SW 0.8546, SXO 0.9108), and bad-hole intervals include several outside any zone.
- **RW and saturation routing:** RW is LAS metadata (0.0700 ohm.m at 230 °F, corrected along the temperature profile to the 220.4–226.7 °F formation profile here; estimated salinity ~30,489 ppm) (map rw record). No measured formation-water resistivity; Dual Water needs a supplied RWB and was not estimated — 1468 shaly carbonate samples in the whole well used Simandoux with RWB set to use Dual Water (map saturation record). Saturation is auto-Archie/Simandoux on RDEP with routing at the 0.3 VCL cutoff.
- **Calibration:** the core model r² = 0.55 (PHIT_CC = 1.136·PHIT − 0.039, n = 200 plugs) (map core_correction record), so PHIE_CC / PERM_CC carry several orders of magnitude of plausible error. PERM_CC exists on only 437/768 samples; PERM (Coates–Denoo, uncalibrated) fills elsewhere. RQI/FZI fall back to PERM where PERM_CC is missing (144 of 576 samples here) (map rock_quality record).
- **SXO/MHI limitation:** no RXO curve; SXO in this interval comes from RSHA at 358 samples; 410 samples sit at SXO = SW = 1. The whole-well record shows 92 SW^(1/5) fallbacks log-wide, none listed for this interval (map saturation record).
- **Transform diagnostic:** least-squares log10(RDEP) vs log10(PHIE) over zones pooled gives slope 0.2676, intercept 0.9895, **R² = 0.0173** (n = 421 of 427) — descriptive only, not a calibration. 132 of 768 PHIE samples are excluded by the log10 transform (limits, Point 1).
- **Missing curves:** DTS and RXO are not measured; MHI is not synthesized beyond SW/SXO (method); SWIRR_APP from Buckles has values on 636/768 samples only (map permeability record).
- **What the logs cannot show:** fluid contacts, producibility and hydrocarbon presence remain log-indicated only; no well test, fluid sample, or pressure data supports confirmation.

## What the logs say and what they cannot

The Brent Group is **porous (PHIE_CC ~0.13–0.22 v/v in sand bodies)** but **water-saturated in most of its volume** (SW mean 0.75, median 1, BVW up to 0.2463 v/v zones pooled). A **subset of cleaner sand packages — Z4, Z5, Z7, with parts of Z1 and Z9 — carries SW below 0.7 and is flagged as POTENTIAL_PAY / PRIME_OIL_TARGET on 24–30% of samples**. The flags are pay/reservoir-quality screens with good log agreement within the clean-sand windows (PERM_CC mean 342.4819 mD pooled zoned; EXCELLENT_RESERVOIR concentrated in the same zones), but they are **not production-demonstrating**: saturation rests on metadata RW, most flushed-zone samples are water-bearing (SXO = SW = 1), gas evidence is weak-tier only, and geophysical gas indicators are uncomputable for lack of DTS. Read the flagging as: **plausible hydrocarbon reservoir in Z4–Z5 and Z7, water-bearing everywhere else, confirmation required**.

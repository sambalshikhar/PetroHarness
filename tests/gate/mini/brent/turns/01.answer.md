# Porosity, Saturation and Hydrocarbon Indicators — Brent Group, Well 30/6-5 (2841.02–2957.91 m)

**Direct answer.** The Brent Group in well 30/6-5 spans 2841.0193–2957.9102 m (gross 116.89 m, 768 samples; 20 lithology zones cover 83.36 m, 71% of the interval). Over the 567 zoned samples the mean total porosity (PHIT) is **0.1988 v/v**, the core-calibrated effective porosity (PHIE_CC) is **0.1254 v/v**, mean water saturation in the uninvaded zone (SW) is **0.6929 v/v**, and flushed-zone water saturation (SXO) is **0.8013 v/v**. Hydrocarbons are *not confirmed*. The pay-related analyzer screens flag 28.4% of the zoned thickness as PRIME_OIL_TARGET, but those are reservoir-quality screens over the water fraction, not fluid typing: the geophysical gas/crossover screen returns zero indications (100% NO_INDICATION, CROSSOVER_FLAG on 0 of 768 samples), the gas-indicator analysis finds only weak effect on 1.9% of the thickness, and 60.8% of the zoned thickness carries NO_INDICATION in the petrophysical classifier. What is below saturation is concentrated in three crossbedded sand/sand zones (Z4, Z5, Z7).

Three separate analyzers disagree in strength: the petrophysical classifier finds pockets of POTENTIAL_PAY / LOW_SW / MOVABLE_HC on 30–31% of zoned thickness, the integrated hierarchy promotes 28.4% of zoned thickness to PRIME_OIL_TARGET on reservoir quality plus pay flag, but the geophysical screen is uniformly negative (100% NO_INDICATION, CROSSOVER_FLAG 0 of 768 samples) and gas-indicator analysis finds weak effect only on 1.64 m.

## Point 5 — Computability of the indicators

**Finding.** The hydrocarbon indicators are *partly* computable and mostly degraded. Geophysical gas typing is effectively non-functional for this well; saturation calibration to core is weak; MHI and SXO exist only where shallow resistivity reaches 494 samples.

**Missing curve.** DTS is not in the input. The VP_VS_RATIO is calculated only when both DTS and DTC are present in plausible slowness ranges; none is added. With DTC present (median DTE/DTC ratio 0.96) but DTS absent, the geophysical hydrocarbon analyzer has no shear-sonic branch and returns NO_INDICATION over 100% of the zoned thickness.

**Core vs log calibration.** SWC has values at 74 of 768 samples; SOC at 44. Whole-interval log-vs-core correlation: R² = 0.1243, bias +0.2245 (log − core), RMSE 0.3742, line log = 0.706·core + 0.3663. Zone-level: only Z5 (n=4, R² 0.703) and Z9 (n=3, R² 0.9269) show usable core agreement; Z1 (R² 0.002), Z7 (0.159), Z11 (0.068), Z15 (0.010) do not. So core SWC/SOC cannot validate the log SW across the Brent Group.

**MHI and SXO availability.** MHI has values at 635 of 768 samples (Z17 has none). SXO is from shallow resistivity at 494 samples, falling back to the fifth-root quicklook at 92; the rest have no flushed-zone saturation. This is why 274 invaded samples collapse to SXO = 1 / SW = 1 and why MOVABLE_HC is assessed on a minority of samples.

**What the well lacks for a firmer answer.** No DTS (no VP/VS gas typing), sparse core SWC/SOC (74 and 44 samples), unreliable log-core saturation calibration (r² ≤ 0.93 only in Z5/Z9), and placeholder zeros in PHIE/CC/PHI_SEC/VCL/SW/SXO. Gas plugs are not identified among the 199 core curves, and the well has no synthetic gas response. The hydrocarbon conclusion rests on resistivity-based SW, a water-bound maturity flag set, and reservoir-quality screens — all log-indicated, none proven.

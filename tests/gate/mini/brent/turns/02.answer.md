# Best Reservoir Quality and Log-vs-Core Permeability in the Brent Group (2841.02–2957.91 m, 30_6-5)

## Answer

By the quality indicators RQI, FZI and PERM_CC, **Z19** (2951.66–2955.01 m, SAND_GROUP_12) is the best zone in the Brent Group: RQI mean **2.4187 µm**, FZI mean **9.2246 µm**, PERM_CC mean **1627.6 mD**, VCL mean **0.193**, PHIE_CC mean **0.1967 v/v** (zone table). Z19 has no KH_CORE plugs. The integrated hydrocarbon analyzer classifies Z19 as 100% WATER_BEARING with SW mean **1.0**.

Where core evidence exists, **Z5** (2870.28–2875.16 m, CROSSBEDDED SAND_GROUP_6) ranks best: RQI **1.6749**, FZI **5.5333**, PERM_CC **864.6 mD**, KH_CORE mean **1894.96 mD** over n=10 plugs, VCL **0.0557**, SW **0.314** (zone table). **Z7** (2877.60–2890.85 m, SAND_GROUP_3) is the next core-verified excellent zone: RQI **1.2698**, FZI **4.4263**, PERM_CC **416.0 mD**, KH_CORE mean **1377.9 mD** over n=34 plugs, PRIME_OIL_TARGET on 97.7% of its samples (zone table).

Uncalibrated Coates-Denoo PERM sits below KH_CORE in **86.5%** of the 384 paired samples; the pooled difference is **−600.4038 mD** (PERM mean 77.3715 mD, KH_CORE mean 677.7752 mD). ## Point 5 — Controls on the permeability gap

1. **Weak core transform and PERM_CC eligibility.** PERM_CC is built from the whole-well transform PHIT_CC = 1.136·PHIT − 0.039 fitted to 198 plugs with r² = 0.55 (workflow record). It is written only where VCL ≤ 0.30 (437 of 768 samples). PERM_CC spans 6.3 orders of magnitude (min **0.0037 mD** at 2938.40 m, max **5261.2 mD** at 2954.25 m).
2. **Coates-Denoo route.** Uncalibrated PERM uses k = 10000·PHIE⁴·((1−SWIRR)/SWIRR)² with SWIRR_APP = BVWirr/PHIE (BVWirr = **0.0638** median) and a clip at 10000 mD (workflow record). It reads much lower than the plugs because it is empirical and porosity-driven, while the plugs are direct measurements.
3. **Bad hole.** Z2 (**88.9%** of its samples) and Z3 (**65%**) sit in bad hole where pad tools read mud, weakening PHIT/PHIE and SW there (earlier result, text). PERM in Z1 sits below its KH_CORE (37.4 vs 0.4786). Z2 has no PERM_CC at all.
4. **Clay volume.** zones pooled: CLEAN_FORMATION (VCL < 0.25) **60.9%** (Z1, Z4–Z5, Z7, Z9, Z13–Z15, Z19), SHALY_FORMATION (VCL > 0.5) **23.8%** (Z2–Z3, Z10–Z12, Z17–Z18), MODERATE_CLAY **15.4%** (Z6, Z8…). The cleanest zone, Z5 (VCL **0.0557**), has the highest KH_CORE and best RQI.
5. **Porosity and saturation.** Z5 and Z7 pair high PHIE_CC with low SW, reinforcing the higher calibrated permeability; the water-bearing, near-zero-porosity silty zones (Z10–Z12) read PERM ≈ 0 (zone table).

## Limitations

- **Model outputs and fallbacks:** PERM, PERM_CC, PHIE, PHIE_CC, RQI, FZI and KH are model outputs or measurements; FZI/RQI fall back to raw PERM at 144 samples where PERM_CC is absent. The core transform is weak (r² = 0.55, n=200). The EXCELLENT_RESERVOIR class inherits that weakness, and its VCL ≤ 0.30 restriction means Z2–Z3, Z17 and others carry no calibrated quality reading.
- **Missing calibration in key zones:** Z19 has no KH_CORE plugs, so its top RQI/FZI/PERM_CC cannot be core-verified and stands apart from Z5/Z7 only when core evidence is required.
- **Fluid and geophysical evidence is log-indicated only.** No DTS or RXO is in the input, so geophysical hydrocarbon indicators give NO_INDICATION on 100%. MHI rests on SXO in this interval, which comes from shallow resistivity (RSHA over RDEP) at 358 of its 768 samples; the SW^(1/5) quicklook is not used here. The hydrocarbon classes (PRIME_OIL_TARGET, WATER_BEARING) come from NET_PAY/excursion cutoffs, not fluid measurements.
- **VCL caveats:** VCL = min(GR, N-D) with GR endpoints picked over the whole log (clean 20–21.6 API, shale ≥100 API). The shale measurement itself carries those picks; SP baselines were found mostly to drift (workflow record).
- **Depths/thicknesses:** The 20 lithology zones cover 83.36 m of the 116.89 m Brent Group gross interval; 201 samples fall outside any zone and are excluded from the zone tables.

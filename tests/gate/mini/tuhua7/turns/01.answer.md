Across the five clay-rich zones (Z2–Z6, 604.57–873.86 m), **total porosity (PHIT) averages 0.266–0.310 v/v** and **effective porosity (PHIE) averages 0.067–0.112 v/v**, the effective total being dragged down by mean shale volume of **0.75–0.87 v/v**. Water saturation (SW) from the archie+indonesia model averages **0.705–0.815 v/v** by zone (pooled 0.7701); the flushed-zone SXO exists only as the quicklook fallback **SW^(1/5)**[map]. **None of the zoned intervals is hydrocarbon pay**: NET_PAY is 0.0% inside Z1–Z6. The only samples meeting the pay cutoffs (VCL<0.3, PHIE>0.08, SW<0.7) lie outside the zoned interval in **0.7616 m at 626.0592–626.6688 m**. Hydrocarbon indicators classify the zoned thickness overwhelmingly as **NO_INDICATION** (petrophysical) and **MARGINAL/NON_RESERVOIR** (integrated), but these are threshold screens resting on model outputs, missing DTS, and a quicklook MHI; they do not establish hydrocarbon presence.

## 2. Water Saturation Character

**Mean-level saturation is uniformly high above 0.7.** Zone means are Z2 0.7008, Z3 0.7785, Z4 0.7501, Z5 0.8150, Z6 0.7050, pooled 0.7701. However, **this holds only at the mean level**, not sample-by-sample: Z2’s p10 is 0.4822 and its minimum 0.3928, so a substantial lower tail dips well below 0.7; Z6’s p10 is 0.5482. The petrophysical indicator scan marks 2.35% of zoned thickness as LOW_SW (SW<0.50) — 6.51 m. Outside the zoned interval, 33 samples show a lower mean SW of 0.5737. Thus the formation is predominantly water-saturated at the zone-mean level, with partial low-SW excursions that do not form pay.

## 4. Hydrocarbon Indicators and Their Confidence

**Petrophysical indicators.** The analyzer’s classes are LOW_SW (criterion SW < 0.50) and NO_INDICATION (background, no criterion met). LOW_SW covers 6.51 m (2.35% of zoned thickness); NO_INDICATION covers 271 m (97.7%). Every zone’s mean class is NO_INDICATION, though Z2 has 15.7% of samples in LOW_SW and Z6 has 5.3%.

**Integrated hydrocarbon potential.** Classes are MARGINAL (all other combinations) and NON_RESERVOIR (explicitly poor reservoir quality). MARGINAL occupies 54% of zoned thickness (150 m); NON_RESERVOIR 46% (128 m). Zone-level means: Z1 MARGINAL, Z2 MARGINAL, Z3 MARGINAL, Z4 MARGINAL, Z5 NON_RESERVOIR, Z6 MARGINAL. Because pay is zero and reservoir quality is poor (high VCL, low PHIE), MARGINAL is a weak screen reflecting marginal reservoir quality rather than hydrocarbon presence.

**Geophysical indicators.** The geophysical analyzer reports **NO_INDICATION across 100% of the zoned thickness** (278 m). Its criterion for a positive crossover requires clean, porous, good-hole rock (VCL < 0.35, PHIE > 0.05, PHID − PHIN > 0.05, BAD_HOLE < 0.5)[map]. Here VCL is 0.75–0.87, effective porosity rarely exceeds 0.08, and the hole is poor in parts (Z6 BAD_HOLE ~57%, Z2 ~20.7%) [map]. DTS shear-sonic is **missing**, so the Vp/Vs-style screening could not be computed [map]. The NO_INDICATION class is therefore a **default screen**, not a measured negative result.

**Movability.** MHI is listed as an analyzer output but is **not computed by the workflow**; it is a quicklook restatement of SW (MHI = SW^0.8) driven by the SXO fallback[map]. Consequently, MHI adds no independent evidence of hydrocarbon movability. INVASION_RATIO (Rxo/Rt) averages 1.0606 with a median of 1.0123 and is minimal in 82.7% of valid samples [map]. There is no flushed zone and resistivity separation is minor. Hydrocarbon presence from resistivity or indicator analysis alone is **not established**[map].

## 5. Limitations and Data Gaps

- **Z1 (590.55–604.4184 m)** has no lithology data; PHIT, PHIE, VCL, SW, SXO, MHI, and NET_PAY are uncomputed over its 92 samples. Any class or saturation reading there rests on background defaults.
- **PHIE distribution is compressed toward zero**: p10 values range 0.033–0.09 across the clastic zones, and many samples record PHIE = 0.
- **Bad hole conditions** in Z2 (20.7%) and Z6 (57.4%) degrade density and neutron porosity consistency [map]. PHID and PHIN are widely separated (e.g., Z3 mean PHID 0.1963 vs PHIN 0.3955). No core calibration (PHIE_CC, PERM_CC) exists [map].
- **SXO is a quicklook transform**, not a flushed-zone observation; it holds no information about invasion beyond SW[map].
- **Missing logs that would firm up the answer**: DTS (shear sonic), RXO/RSHA/RESS finite flushed-zone resistivity, and core porosity/permeability calibration. Without them, cross-bedding, Vp/Vs ratios, and independent pay calibration are absent [map].
- **Model outputs**: all porosity curves (PHIT, PHIE, PHID, PHIN, PHIS, PHI_SEC), SXO, and NET_PAY are workflow model outputs; SXO and MHI are fallback or quicklook forms[map]. Where they disagree or rest on defaults, the reading is downgraded accordingly.

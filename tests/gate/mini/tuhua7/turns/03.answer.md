The saturation step used **RW = formation water resistivity at formation temperature, ohm.m**, with per-sample RW values from the `rw` step (pooled mean **0.06449 ohm.m** across Z2–Z6, range **0.06306–0.06593 ohm.m**) applied through the archie+indonesia saturation model over the 604.57–873.86 m interval.

The saturation record runs `1/sqrt(Rt) = [VCL^(1-VCL/2)/sqrt(Rcl) + PHIE^(m/2)/sqrt(a*Rw)] * Sw^(n/2)`, so it consumed the RW curve directly; the RW step was `rwa_clean_wet` fallback because Rmf was unavailable, using P5 of RDEP·PHIE^m/a where VCL < 0.3 and PHIE > 0.05, then corrected for temperature from the measured TEMP log (formation temp 108.99–156.16 °F). RW is therefore model-derived, not a measured resistivity.

Trust limit: no BHT anchor was found, so the geothermal gradient (30 °C/km) and fallback Rwa heuristics carry more uncertainty into RW and hence SW.

Readings and their weaknesses: a caveat (a curve computed from another, a quicklook input, a model's assumptions, thin beds, bad hole) lowers the grade of a reading; it does not turn the workflow's classes or flags into a denial. A negative claim (no relationship, not distinct, not a control, none) needs the zone-level count: in how many zones the pattern holds and in how many it breaks. Whether one property indicates another is read first from where both occur, zone by zone and in the classes; a sample coefficient within one zone is weakened by the narrow range there and qualifies that reading, it does not decide it. A class that could not be computed (NO_DATA, UNDETERMINED, an absent input) means not assessed here, never 'none'; 'not established' is not 'no evidence'.

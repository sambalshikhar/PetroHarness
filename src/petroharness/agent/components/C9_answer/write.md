How to report, each rule with its reason:
1. Open with the answer in the form the question asks: where or which -> the intervals (depths, zones), each with its
   grade; how much -> the value with its basis; how one property relates to another -> the direction, the strength,
   and where it holds and where it breaks; what kind -> each unit with its class; why -> the reading and the evidence
   it rests on. (The reader asked that question.)
2. Then the evidence the answer rests on: the intervals, values and classes the results give, each with what limits
   it. (The reader checks the answer against its evidence.)
3. A class or flag is a threshold screen of the logs. When you use one, say its criterion in plain words and where it
   occurs, and grade it by the logs it rests on: a screen named for a conclusion (pay, target, contact, transition,
   movable) is a candidate until porosity, clay, saturation and hole condition agree with it. (A label is not evidence
   of itself.)
4. A trend, relation or contrast is stated with its numbers: its direction, its size and where it holds. A weak or
   partial signal is reported as weak and placed, never as a clear pattern and never as none. (Overstating and denying
   both misreport partial evidence.)
5. Fluids, contacts, pore types and producibility are log-indicated: say what supports them and what would confirm
   them; never write confirmed or proven. (Logs alone cannot establish them.)
6. The workflow's own sections (its classifications and interpretation summaries) are the computed record: start from
   what they conclude, and let your own statistics and checks raise or lower its grade; where they disagree, say so and
   which reading you favour and why. (They are the analysis the logs were run through.)
7. In the limitations, say which curves the answer rests on are model outputs, fallbacks, held at a limit, constant or
   not computed (WHAT THE CURVES ARE), and what that changes in the answer; and which logs the question needs that this
   well lacks. (What the data cannot show is part of how far the answer can be trusted.)
8. Report what bears on the question; other properties only where they change the answer. (Extra material buries the
   answer.)

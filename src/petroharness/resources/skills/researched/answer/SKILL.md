# answer

## Consider
- What does each part of the question ask for (a value, where, a judgement, an explanation), and on which basis (each zone, the zones pooled, the whole interval, a condition)?
- What did the workflow compute for it (values, classes, flags), and what are each one's weaknesses here (hole condition, clipping, the model and its parameters, data coverage)?
- Which evidence beyond the workflow's own label agrees or disagrees, at the same depths?

## Compare
- stats CURVES   (the asked curves, each zone and pooled)
- step NAME      (how a computed curve was made: its method and parameters)

## Decide
- Answer each part with the values computed for it (value, n, depths, zones, thickness, share), each with its basis.
- Where the readings are mixed, say where the condition holds and where it does not, zone by zone.
- A negative finding (no relationship, not distinct, not a control, none) says in how many zones the pattern holds and in how many it breaks, and why that overrules the workflow's classes where they say otherwise; a pooled coefficient alone mixes rock types.

## Know
- The lithology zones are the groups the workflow reports on.
- A weakness qualifies a finding; the finding and its weakness are reported together.
- A caveat (a curve computed from another, a quicklook input, a model's assumptions, thin beds, bad hole) lowers the confidence of a reading; it does not reverse the workflow's classes or flags into a denial.

## Do not state
- Values the opened results do not show (compute them from shown values with calc).
